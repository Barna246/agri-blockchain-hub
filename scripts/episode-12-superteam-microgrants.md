# Episode 12: Free Money for Solana Builders, No Pitch Deck Required
Capped under 85 seconds. Unlike the FAO episode, this opportunity has no deadline, so it doesn't go stale the way a one-time application window does.

---

## Full script (~80 sec)

"If you're building anything on Solana, including agriculture tools, there's up to ten thousand dollars sitting in a program you've probably never opened.

It's called Superteam Earn, and unlike most grant programs, it isn't a once-a-year competition. It's a rolling platform: microgrants from zero to ten thousand dollars in USDC, aimed specifically at early-stage builders in emerging markets, including Africa. No pitch deck marathon, no annual deadline to miss.

I'm SuperteamNG's Ogun State Moderator, so I've watched this ecosystem from the inside. Most people building agri-tech in Nigeria have never even opened Superteam Earn, because they don't think of themselves as 'Web3 builders.' If your tool touches Solana at any layer, payments, data logging, tokenized assets, you qualify to look.

This isn't a one-time application window like the other funding episodes I've covered. It's open right now, and it'll still be open next month. Check Superteam Earn's own platform for live listings before you assume you're not eligible."
