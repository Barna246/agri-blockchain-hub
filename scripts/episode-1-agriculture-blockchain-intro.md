# Episode 1: Agriculture x Blockchain — What It Actually Is
Script draft for tonight. Runs ~85-90 seconds at a natural pace.

## Full script (~85-90 sec)

**HOOK — 0:00-0:08**
"In December 2016, a farmer in Australia delivered a truck of wheat — and got paid the second it hit the scale. No bank. No invoice. No two-week wait. That's blockchain in agriculture. And for the next few weeks, I'm breaking down exactly what that means — especially for us."

**WHAT IT ACTUALLY IS — 0:08-0:32**
"Here's the boring truth first: documenting a single trade transaction can cost about 7% of the value of the goods being traded. That's before anything even ships. Blockchain doesn't fix farming. It fixes the paperwork, the trust, and the waiting *around* farming — payments, land records, proof your rice is actually organic, proof a cooperative didn't shortchange you."

**THE EXAMPLE — 0:32-0:52**
"That wheat farmer I mentioned — a company called AgriDigital ran the whole trade on a blockchain. The moment his grain was weighed, a smart contract paid him instantly. Normal payment terms in that industry run two to five *weeks*. That's not a crypto pitch. That's just... faster paperwork."

**WHY IT MATTERS HERE — 0:52-1:15**
"Now bring that home. Less than 10% of land in Sub-Saharan Africa is even formally documented. Smallholder farmers lose value to middlemen, bad records, and cooperatives they can't fully verify. That's the actual problem blockchain is being tested against — in Kenya, in Ghana, and now in Nigeria too."

**SERIES TEASE + CTA — 1:15-1:30**
"This series is going to walk through the real projects, the real numbers, and the real limits — including when blockchain is the wrong tool for the job. Follow along. First deep dive drops soon."
